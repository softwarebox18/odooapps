Salesman User Group and Access Control Base Module (Odoo18)

This module introduces a Salesman user group to manage access rights and permissions for users involved in Sales, Inventory, Product, Accounting, and Warehouse operations.

It serves as a base module that can be reused by other custom or business modules to provide consistent access control for salesman-level users across your Odoo environment.

With this module, system administrators can easily assign the Salesman group to users and control what they can view, create, write, or delete in critical business models such as Sales Orders, Products, Stock Pickings, Warehouses, and Accounting Reports.

Key Features

  • Predefined Salesman Group:Automatically creates a Salesman group under the Sales category.
  • Access Control Setup:Provides controlled read/write/create permissions for core business models — Sales, Stock, Product, Account, and UoM.
  • Ready for Extension:Can be used as a base module for your other custom modules to ensure consistent group access management.
  • Secure Data Handling:Restricts users from deleting or modifying restricted business data.
  • Compatible with Multiple Modules:Supports sale_management, stock, product, account, mail, and uom.

How to Configure

  • Simply install the module from the Odoo app store, and the new functionality will be added.
  • Navigate to Settings, Users & Companies, Users.
  • Open a user form and assign the Salesman group (Salesman under Sales category).
  • Once assigned, the user will automatically receive predefined access permissions related to:
    • Sales Orders
    • Stock Transfers and Warehouses
    • Products and Pricelists
    • Accounting and Reports
    • Units of Measure and Mail Activities
